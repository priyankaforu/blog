---
title: "Gpt can never be my German Speaking Partner"
date: 2026-10-30
tags: ["personal", "life"]
---

I have been practising German on and off for the last 2 months now, and I do have a speaking partner who connects with me every evening in CET. For the last one week he was so busy and I was less motivated to learn those new nouns and verbs

the best way to learn any new language is to live with it , to think in that language .. so AI is more advanced right, and AI even can teach the language. We don't need any language teachers to help us with the language rules . Yes I thought the same , opened GPT for the 3 continuous days to practice the language, started talking to it and asked it to correct me. On the fourth day I became lazy to repeat this drill and AI was never upset when I said no to it.

But my speaking partner was unlike GPT, I have to think if he will be upset. If I say I cannot make it today, he thinks that I don't have commitment towards my goal. So I have to think twice to say no to him. When I don't his level of language then I believe that I didn't learn at his pace. I feel that I am not working hard enough to keep up. And ofcourse he is not the native German speaker , neither am I , we talk lot of mistakes (I do more than him though) and there is no one to correct us. As most of you think,  we should not be worried about it because we have AI that can do anything right ? yes if we can record our conversation after we talk AI can correct it pretty well, but AI can never be my speaking partner who gets upset, who gets anxious and who gets dull , angry whenever things won't work and more over I can never say no to him because I feel bad if he thinks that I don't care my goal of learning German and that said AI can never be that human with whom I interact with all my emotions
